6C

6C — Lots of Practice6C——大量练习

16 blocks · 16 个内容块Source 8f0e8121Original

What You're Doing你正在做什么

You've built all the skills, filled the vocabulary gaps, and developed automaticity. This sub-phase is about reinforcing everything through volume. There's nothing new to learn here — just a lot of doing.

你已经建立了各项技能，填补了词汇缺口，也发展出了自动化能力。这个子阶段的目标，是通过大量练习巩固一切。这里没有新东西要学——只需要大量实践。

Think of this as the final consolidation. Everything you've built over hundreds (or thousands) of hours needs to be practiced until it's truly second nature. The more varied your practice, the more robust your fluency becomes.

把它看作最后的巩固阶段。你在数百（甚至数千）小时中建立的一切，都需要不断练习，直到真正变成本能。你的练习越多样化，流利能力就越稳固。

What's New有什么新内容

There are no new activities. The shift is toward high-volume, varied practice across all skills:

没有新的活动。变化在于，所有技能都要进行高强度、多样化的练习：

Freeflow dominates. The majority of your time is spent consuming and producing the language naturally — reading, listening, speaking, and writing in contexts that feel like normal life rather than study. Now's the best time to get into audiobooks, long-form podcasts or long book series. Freeflow Immersion

自由流活动占主导。你大部分时间都在自然地接收和产出语言——在接近日常生活而非学习场景的语境中阅读、听、说、写。现在正是开始接触有声书、长篇播客或长篇系列书籍的最佳时机。自由流沉浸

Video Creation — Creating polished content in the target language (writing a script, recording, editing) exercises every skill at once. It's an excellent capstone activity for this phase because it combines reading, writing, speaking, and self-analysis. Video Creation

视频创作——用目标语言创作精良内容（编写脚本、录制、剪辑）能同时锻炼所有技能。这是本阶段非常出色的收尾活动，因为它结合了阅读、写作、口语和自我分析。视频创作

Continue conversations and writing. Speak with partners regularly, write consistently, and keep pushing yourself into new and challenging situations in the language.

继续进行对话和写作。定期与语伴交流，坚持写作，并不断让自己置身于语言学习中更新鲜、更具挑战性的情境。

Your Routine你的日常安排

Freeflow is the biggest block, with output remaining substantial: The Pillars of Language Learning

自由流式沉浸占比最大，同时保持大量输出：语言学习的支柱

  • Priming: 10%
  • Interactive Immersion: 10%
  • Freeflow Immersion: 50%
  • Output: 30%
  • 预热：10%
  • 互动式沉浸：10%
  • 自由流式沉浸：50%
  • 输出：30%

Sample Daily Schedule每日安排示例

  • Vocab study: 10 mins
  • Sentence Mining: 10 mins
  • Freeflow Immersion (reading or listening): 50 mins
  • Video Creation or Speaking/Writing practice: 30 mins
  • 词汇学习：10分钟
  • 句子挖掘：10分钟
  • 自由流式沉浸（阅读或听力）：50分钟
  • 视频创作或口语/写作练习：30分钟

When to Move On何时进入下一阶段

Move to Phase 7 when you:

当你满足以下条件时，进入第7阶段：

  • Can write, correct, and record a 10+ minute video on a complex topic with clear expression of ideas and opinions, without any major unnoticed errors
  • Can have a 60+ minute conversation without needing to ask for common words or having your partner ask you to rephrase more than 3 times
  • Feel that using the language is no longer effortful — it's become a normal part of your life
  • 能够围绕复杂主题写作、修改并录制一个10分钟以上的视频，清晰表达自己的想法和观点，且没有任何未被察觉的重大错误
  • 能够进行60分钟以上的对话，不需要询问常见词汇，也不会让语伴要求你改述超过3次
  • 觉得使用这门语言不再费力——它已经成为生活中自然而然的一部分
