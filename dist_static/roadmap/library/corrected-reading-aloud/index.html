Corrected Reading Aloud有纠正的朗读

9 blocks · 9 个内容块Source 1dbba4e3Original

Corrected reading aloud is the same as uncorrected reading aloud, but with feedback from a tutor, native speaker, or tool that helps you identify and fix pronunciation errors.

有纠正的朗读与无纠正的朗读相同，但会获得导师、母语者或工具提供的反馈，帮助你发现并纠正发音错误。

Uncorrected Reading Aloud

无纠正的朗读

How It Differs from Uncorrected它与无纠正的朗读有何不同

In uncorrected reading aloud, you just practice the physical act of speaking. In corrected reading aloud, someone (or something) is listening and pointing out specific sounds, words, or patterns you're getting wrong. This makes it more targeted and effective for improving pronunciation accuracy.

进行无纠正的朗读时，你只是在练习开口说话这一身体动作。进行有纠正的朗读时，会有人（或某种工具）聆听，并指出你读错的具体音、词语或发音模式。这种练习更有针对性，也能更有效地提高发音准确度。

How to Do It如何练习

  • Read aloud during a tutoring session and have your tutor correct your pronunciation in real-time
  • Record yourself reading aloud, then have a native speaker or AI tool analyze the recording
  • Use speech-to-text tools — words that get transcribed incorrectly are probably being mispronounced or said unnaturally
  • 在辅导课上朗读，请导师实时纠正你的发音
  • 录下自己的朗读，再请母语者或 AI 工具分析录音
  • 使用语音转文字工具——被错误转写的词，很可能是你发音不准或说得不自然

Uncorrected Reading Aloud

无纠正的朗读

When to Start何时开始

Corrected reading aloud is introduced in Phase 4B and continues to be useful through Phase 7.

有纠正的朗读从阶段 4B 开始引入，并会一直到阶段 7 都很有用。
