Sentence Mining句子挖掘

25 blocks · 25 个内容块Source 1b9a5a8cOriginal

Sentence mining is the process of finding sentences with unknown words in your immersion content, looking up the words, and saving them as flashcards for later review. It's one of the most powerful vocabulary-building techniques in the Refold method because you're learning words in context, from content you actually enjoy.

句子挖掘是指从沉浸内容中找出包含生词的句子，查询这些词，并将句子保存成闪卡，供日后复习。它是 Refold 方法中最强大的词汇积累技巧之一，因为你是在自己真正喜欢的内容中结合语境学习词汇。

-> 句子挖掘教程

How It Works具体方法

The basic loop:

基本流程如下：

  1. You're watching a TV show (or video, or reading) with target-language subtitles
  2. You encounter a sentence where you understand everything except 1-2 words
  3. You pause and look up the unknown word(s)
  4. You make sure you understand the whole sentence
  5. You save the sentence as a flashcard (usually with audio and/or an image)
  6. You rewatch the section to hear it at natural speed
  7. Continue
  1. 观看带目标语言字幕的电视节目或视频，或者进行阅读
  2. 遇到一个除了 1～2 个词之外其余部分都能理解的句子
  3. 暂停并查询不认识的词
  4. 确认自己理解整个句子
  5. 将句子保存成闪卡（通常附带音频和／或图片）
  6. 重新观看这一片段，以自然语速再听一遍
  7. 继续观看或阅读

Later, during your vocab study time, you review these cards using spaced repetition (Anki or similar).

之后，在词汇学习时间里，使用间隔重复（Anki 或类似工具）复习这些卡片。

Why It's So Effective为什么如此有效

Sentence mining combines several things that make vocabulary stick:

句子挖掘结合了多种有助于牢固记忆词汇的因素：

  • Context: You learn words in real sentences, not isolated lists
  • Personal relevance: The words come from content you chose and enjoy
  • The Ikea effect: Words and cards you make yourself are easier to learn and feel more personal
  • Multi-channel: Cards often include audio, images, and text from the original scene
  • Spaced repetition: Regular review with increasing intervals cements the words
  • Positive feedback loop: The more words you learn, the more you understand, which makes mining more enjoyable
  • 语境：你是在真实句子中学习词汇，而不是背诵孤立的词表
  • 个人相关性：这些词来自你亲自选择并喜欢的内容
  • 宜家效应：自己找到的词和亲手制作的卡片更容易学习，也更有个人意义
  • 多通道：卡片通常包含原场景中的音频、图像和文字
  • 间隔重复：以逐渐延长的间隔定期复习，能巩固词汇记忆
  • 正向反馈循环：学会的词越多，能理解的内容就越多，句子挖掘也会变得更有趣

What to Mine挖掘什么

Look for "low-hanging fruit" — sentences where you already understand most of the words and only need to learn one or two new ones. These are sometimes called "1T sentences" (one target).

寻找“容易摘到的果实”——也就是你已经理解其中大部分单词、只需学习一两个新词的句子。这类句子有时也称为“1T 句子”（只有一个目标词）。

Do mine: Sentences with 1 (or maybe 2) unknown words that seem useful or interesting.

适合挖掘：只有 1 个（最多 2 个）未知词，而且看起来实用或有趣的句子。

Don't mine: Sentences with 3+ unknown words (you don't even understand the sentence), words that are extremely niche or unlikely to appear again, or words you already sort of know.

不适合挖掘：包含 3 个以上未知词的句子（你甚至无法理解整句话）、极其小众或不太可能再次出现的词，以及你已经大致认识的词。

Common Mistakes常见错误

  • Mining too many cards. It's easy to save 30+ cards in a session, but if you can't review them all, they pile up. Delete cards that aren't useful rather than letting them create a backlog.
  • Mining words you already know. If you saved a word, then realize you know it when it comes up in your review, delete the card. Don't waste review time on known words.
  • Only mining, never freeflowing. Mining is interactive immersion. You still need freeflow time to build speed and automaticity.
  • 挖掘太多卡片。一次学习很容易保存 30 多张卡片，但如果无法全部复习，它们就会越积越多。对于没有用的卡片，直接删除，不要让它们形成积压。
  • 挖掘已经认识的词。如果你保存了一个词，却在复习时发现自己其实认识它，就删除这张卡片。不要把复习时间浪费在已知词上。
  • 只做句子挖掘，从不进行自由沉浸。句子挖掘属于互动式沉浸。你仍然需要留出自由沉浸的时间，以提升处理速度并培养自动化能力。

Tools工具

The specific tools depend on your platform and language, but the general setup involves: a media player with subtitle support, a popup dictionary, and a flashcard tool (usually Anki).

具体工具取决于你使用的平台和学习的语言，但一般需要：支持字幕的媒体播放器、划词弹出词典，以及闪卡工具（通常是 Anki）。

Many learners use browser extensions or dedicated apps that streamline the process. The setup can be frustrating at first, but once it's working, the workflow becomes smooth.

许多学习者会使用浏览器扩展或专用应用来简化这一流程。刚开始配置时可能令人沮丧，但一旦设置妥当，整个流程就会变得很顺畅。

When to Start何时开始

Sentence mining is introduced in Phase 2B and remains useful through Phase 7. In Phase 3+, you'll also do "sentence mining while listening" — the same concept, but trying to understand through audio first before revealing subtitles.

句子挖掘会在阶段 2B 引入，并一直到阶段 7 都很有用。从阶段 3 开始，你还会进行“边听边做句子挖掘”——概念相同，但需要先尝试通过音频理解，遇到困难后才显示字幕。

Research and Reasoning研究与原理

Sentence mining combines several evidence-based principles of vocabulary acquisition. The effectiveness of spaced repetition is well-established: Nakata (2015) compared expanding and equal spacing schedules and found that both significantly outperform massed practice.

句子挖掘结合了多项有实证依据的词汇习得原则。间隔重复的有效性早已得到充分证实：Nakata（2015）比较了逐渐延长间隔和固定间隔的复习安排，发现两者都明显优于集中练习。

The research distinction between deliberate and incidental vocabulary learning is also crucial. Webb (2020) showed that incidental learning through reading, listening, and viewing is a primary driver of vocabulary growth, particularly for building rich, contextual word knowledge. However, Nation (2001) demonstrates that deliberate study (like flashcards) serves an important complementary function — it increases noticing of target words when they appear in subsequent input, accelerating the acquisition process.

研究中对刻意词汇学习与附带词汇学习的区分也至关重要。Webb（2020）指出，通过阅读、听力和观看内容进行的附带学习，是词汇量增长的主要动力，尤其有助于建立丰富且有语境的词汇知识。不过，Nation（2001）表明，刻意学习（如使用闪卡）也发挥着重要的补充作用——它会让学习者在后续输入中更容易注意到目标词，从而加快习得过程。

The context-based approach in sentence mining is supported by research on depth of processing. Hulstijn (2001) showed that learners remember words better when they process them with greater cognitive effort and contextual engagement. Sentence mining forces exactly this kind of deep processing by anchoring words in real sentences from meaningful content, rather than learning them in isolation.

句子挖掘以语境为基础的方法，得到了有关加工深度研究的支持。Hulstijn（2001）指出，当学习者投入更多认知努力并结合语境处理单词时，对单词的记忆会更加牢固。句子挖掘把单词固定在有意义内容中的真实句子里，而不是孤立地学习单词，恰好促使学习者进行这种深度加工。
