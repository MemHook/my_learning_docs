The Refold AppRefold 应用

8 blocks · 8 个内容块Source 930fadc7Original

The Refold App is a tool designed to help you track your language learning activities, build consistent habits, and monitor your progress through the Refold roadmap.

Refold 应用是一款帮助你记录语言学习活动、养成稳定习惯，并在 Refold 路线图中跟踪学习进度的工具。

If you're reading this, you already have an account and are on the webapp! You can click the "Dashboard" button in the header to see your main tracking page.

既然你正在阅读这段内容，就说明你已经拥有账户并进入了网页应用！你可以点击页眉中的“仪表板”按钮，查看主要的学习记录页面。

You can also download the mobile app if you'd like.

如果愿意，你也可以下载移动应用。

This video predates the webapp! But the main functions are the same. Hopefully, it's fairly intuitive.

这段视频早于网页应用！但主要功能是一样的。希望它的使用方式足够直观。

Key Features主要功能

  • Activity tracking: Log your daily learning time by activity type (priming, interactive, freeflow, output)
  • Activity Library: If you don't know what to do or what to call your studying, the Library has dozens of in-depth tutorials
  • Habit Health: Track your consistency with a hit-rate model rather than streaks — see Habit Health for details
  • Progress overview: See your cumulative hours and how they compare to the benchmarks for your current phase
  • 活动追踪：按活动类型（准备、互动、自由流、输出）记录每天的学习时间
  • 活动库：如果你不知道该做什么，或不知道如何称呼自己的学习活动，活动库中有数十份深入的教程
  • 习惯健康度：使用命中率模型而不是连续打卡来追踪坚持情况——详情请参阅“习惯健康度”
  • 进度概览：查看累计学习时长，以及它与当前阶段基准值的对比

Tips提示

  • Use the stopwatch whenever possible for accurate tracking, but don't stress about perfect accuracy
  • Track consistently for at least 2 weeks to see meaningful patterns
  • Review your data weekly to check whether your time balance matches the recommendations for your phase
  • 尽可能使用秒表来准确追踪，但不必为完美的准确性焦虑
  • 至少连续追踪 2 周，才能看到有意义的规律
  • 每周查看一次数据，确认你的时间分配是否符合当前阶段的建议
