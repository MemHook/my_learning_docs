Proficiency Testing语言水平测试

7 blocks · 7 个内容块Source b4304a41Original

Proficiency tests are standardized exams that certify your language ability at a specific level. Common tests include CEFR-based exams (DELE for Spanish, DELF/DALF for French, Goethe-Zertifikat for German, etc.), JLPT for Japanese, HSK for Chinese, and TOPIK for Korean.

语言水平测试是用于认证你的语言能力达到特定级别的标准化考试。常见考试包括以 CEFR 为标准的考试（西班牙语 DELE、法语 DELF/DALF、德语 Goethe-Zertifikat 等）、日语 JLPT、汉语 HSK，以及韩语 TOPIK。

Do You Need One?你需要参加吗？

Only if you have a specific reason: university admission, work requirements, immigration, or personal goals. The Refold roadmap provides more actionable guidance for improvement than a test level ever could.

只有在你有明确理由时才需要，例如大学入学、工作要求、移民或个人目标。与任何考试等级相比，Refold 路线图都能为提升语言能力提供更具可操作性的指导。

The Refold AdvantageRefold 的优势

If you've followed the Refold method, you have much stronger listening and reading comprehension than most test-takers at your level. Your main challenges will be test-specific: unfamiliar question formats, timed writing under constraints, and grammar points that are tested but rarely appear in natural immersion.

如果你一直遵循 Refold 方法，那么你的听力和阅读理解能力会远强于同等级的大多数考生。你面临的主要挑战将与考试本身有关：不熟悉的题型、限时且受条件约束的写作，以及考试会考、却很少出现在自然沉浸中的语法点。
