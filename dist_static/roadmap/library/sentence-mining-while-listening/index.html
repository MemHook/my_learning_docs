Sentence Mining (While Listening)句子挖掘（边听边挖掘）

12 blocks · 12 个内容块Source c5af329eOriginal

Sentence Mining while Listening is the same concept as regular sentence mining, but with a twist: you try to understand through audio first, only revealing subtitles when you're stuck.

边听边做句子挖掘与普通句子挖掘的概念相同，但有一点变化：你要先尝试通过音频理解，只有遇到困难时才显示字幕。

Sentence Mining

句子挖掘

How It Works具体做法

  1. Watch content with subtitles hidden (but loaded and ready to reveal)
  2. Listen to each line and try to understand
  3. When you hear something interesting or can't understand, replay it a few times
  4. If you still can't understand, reveal the subtitles and check
  5. Look up unknown words
  6. If the sentence has only 1-2 unknown words, save it as a flashcard
  7. Rewatch the section to hear it at natural speed
  8. Hide subtitles again and continue
  1. 观看内容时将字幕设为隐藏（但要提前加载好，以便随时显示）
  2. 听每一句台词，并尝试理解
  3. 听到有趣的内容或无法理解时，重放几遍
  4. 如果仍然无法理解，就显示字幕进行核对
  5. 查询未知词
  6. 如果句子中只有 1～2 个未知词，就将它保存为闪卡
  7. 重新观看这一片段，听清它在自然语速下的发音
  8. 再次隐藏字幕，然后继续

How It Differs from Regular Sentence Mining与普通句子挖掘有何不同

Regular sentence mining (Phase 2) uses subtitles as the primary channel — you read along and mine words you see. Sentence Mining while Listening flips this: audio is primary, text is the fallback.

普通句子挖掘（阶段 2）以字幕为主要渠道——你一边阅读字幕，一边挖掘看到的单词。边听边做句子挖掘则反过来：音频是主要渠道，文字只在需要时作为辅助。

This trains your ear while still building vocabulary.

这样既能训练听力，又能继续积累词汇。

Tips建议

  • This is harder and slower than text-based mining. That's the point.
  • Use the same tools and setup as regular sentence mining, just keep subtitles hidden by default
  • Auto-pause features are helpful for giving you time to process each line
  • As your listening improves, you'll need to reveal subtitles less and less
  • 这比基于文字的句子挖掘更难、速度也更慢——这正是它的意义所在。
  • 使用与普通句子挖掘相同的工具和配置，只需默认隐藏字幕
  • 自动暂停功能很有帮助，可以让你有时间处理每一句台词
  • 随着听力提高，你需要显示字幕的次数会越来越少

When to Start何时开始

Sentence Mining while Listening is introduced in Phase 3A and continues through Phase 7.

边听边做句子挖掘会在阶段 3A 引入，并持续到阶段 7。
