How Long Does It Take to Learn a Language学会一门语言需要多长时间

78 blocks · 78 个内容块Source aac4ec33Original

That is one of the most commonly asked questions in all of language learning.

这是整个语言学习领域中最常见的问题之一。

People want to know what they're getting into!

人们当然想知道自己将要面对什么！

There are a few different ways to think about "how long it takes to learn a language." But by far the most accurate and useful measurement is "Total hours of effort to reach fluency."

“学会一门语言需要多长时间”可以从几个不同角度来思考。但到目前为止，最准确且最实用的衡量方式是：“达到流利程度所需投入的总小时数。”

While there are issues with even that simple statement (what is fluency? What counts as an hour of effort?), it's still a very useful number to know.

尽管就连这句简单的表述也存在一些问题（怎样才算流利？怎样才算投入一小时？），这个数字仍然非常值得了解。

This article dives into the nuance of these numbers and how to figure out what they mean for you and your target language.

本文将深入探讨这些数字背后的细微差别，并说明如何判断它们对你和你的目标语言意味着什么。

The Language Really Matters语言本身真的很重要

The first hurdle we face when determining the number of hours it takes to learn a language is that different languages take different amounts of time for different people.

要确定学会一门语言需要多少小时，我们面对的第一个难题是：不同的人学习不同语言，所需时间也不同。

Language distance is the most important factor. Basically, how different is your target language from your native language (or another language you already speak)? Categories of Language Difficulty

语言距离是最重要的因素。简单来说，你的目标语言与母语（或你已经会的另一门语言）有多么不同？语言难度分类

If you speak Spanish as your native language, Italian is much easier than Dutch. Italian and Spanish share a massive amount of vocabulary, grammar, and even sounds. But Spanish and Dutch only have a few things in common.

如果西班牙语是你的母语，那么意大利语会比荷兰语容易得多。意大利语和西班牙语共享大量词汇、语法，甚至语音；西班牙语和荷兰语的共同点却很少。

So, we separate languages (roughly) into 4 different groups, depending on how different they are from your native language. Since Refold is a primarily English-speaking company, we use English as the native language for these calculations. But if you speak a different language, your version of this chart will look very different.

因此，我们会根据一门语言与母语的差异程度，大致将语言分成四组。由于 Refold 是一家主要使用英语的公司，我们在这些计算中以英语为母语。但如果你使用另一种语言，你所对应的图表将会大不相同。

Similar languages share many things in common. They have a common linguistic ancestor and share a decent amount of vocabulary and may have similar sentence structures.

相近语言有许多共同之处。它们拥有共同的语言祖先，共享相当多的词汇，句子结构也可能相似。

Distant languages have essentially nothing in common. Maybe a few borrowed words (usually due to English's massive influence in the world), but nothing else. Distant languages also have some things that are very different (such as tones), making them take more time to learn.

远缘语言基本上毫无共同之处。或许会有少量借词（通常是因为英语在全球影响巨大），除此之外就没有了。远缘语言还具有一些差异非常大的特征（例如声调），因此需要更长时间才能学会。

Cousin languages are usually a very small group of languages that are very closely related. These languages share over 50% (often way more) of their vocabulary and have very similar sentence structures and grammar features.

近亲语言通常是一个规模很小、彼此关系极为密切的语言群。这些语言共享 50% 以上（往往远不止于此）的词汇，句子结构和语法特征也非常相似。

For example, the Romance languages (Spanish, Catalan, Italian, French, Portuguese, etc.) or the Slavic languages (Russian, Ukrainian, Polish, Czech, Slovak, etc.).

例如罗曼语族语言（西班牙语、加泰罗尼亚语、意大利语、法语、葡萄牙语等），或斯拉夫语族语言（俄语、乌克兰语、波兰语、捷克语、斯洛伐克语等）。

All other languages fall into a middle category. They're not particularly distant or similar to your native language and require roughly the same amount of time to learn.

其他所有语言都属于中间类别。它们与母语既不算特别疏远，也不算特别相近，学习所需的时间大致相同。

Please keep in mind that "language distance" isn't meant to be a perfect, objective measurement. All language pairs will have slightly different nuance to them. But the language groupings are required if we want to figure out how long it will take.

请记住，“语言距离”并不是一种完美、客观的衡量标准。每一对语言之间都有略微不同的细微差别。不过，如果我们想估算学习需要多长时间，就必须对语言进行分组。

What's Your Experience?你有哪些经验？

The other major factor in learning speed is you! Not only your native language, but how much experience you have with language learning. Have you successfully learned other languages in the past? Did you try to learn your current target language a decade ago?

影响学习速度的另一个主要因素是你自己！不仅要考虑你的母语，还要考虑你有多少语言学习经验。你过去是否成功学会过其他语言？你是否曾在十年前尝试学习现在的目标语言？

All of these factors are important to consider!

这些因素都很值得考虑！

If you've already learned two other foreign languages as an adult, you're going to have a much easier time with a third one, even if it's a very distant language. You already understand your personal learning process and have experienced a lot of the pitfalls.

如果你成年后已经学会了另外两门外语，那么学习第三门时会轻松得多，即使它是一门非常远缘的语言。你已经了解适合自己的学习过程，也经历过许多常见陷阱。

But if this is your first foreign language, you'll be figuring all that out while also learning the language.

但如果这是你的第一门外语，你就需要一边学习这门语言，一边摸索所有这些事情。

This is why we recommend that people learn a more similar language first, as long as they plan on learning more eventually. We see some form of this question all the time:

正因如此，如果一个人最终打算学习多门语言，我们会建议他先学习一门更相近的语言。我们经常看到类似这样的问题：

"Hi! I'm new to language learning. I know that I want to learn Spanish and Japanese. Which one should I learn first as a native English speaker?"

“你好！我是语言学习新手。我想学西班牙语和日语。作为英语母语者，我应该先学哪一门？”

Assuming this person truly wants to learn both, starting with Spanish will allow them to make quicker progress and figure out their own learning style and process. Then, when they're ready to focus on Japanese, they're already a seasoned language learner.

假设这个人确实想把两门语言都学会，那么先学西班牙语能让他更快取得进步，并摸索出自己的学习风格和流程。之后，当他准备好专注于日语时，就已经是一位经验丰富的语言学习者了。

The other important factor here is if you have experience learning a language related to your current target language. An English speaker who's fluent in Spanish will have a much easier time learning Italian than a monolingual English speaker (much like we discussed in the previous section).

另一个重要因素是，你是否有学习与当前目标语言相关语言的经验。一个英语母语者如果能流利使用西班牙语，学习意大利语就会比只会英语的人轻松得多（这与我们在上一节讨论的情况很相似）。

What Are Your Goals?你的目标是什么？

The final, critical factor that needs to be mentioned before getting to the actual hour estimates is your personal goal in the language.

在给出具体小时数估算之前，最后一个必须提到的关键因素是你对这门语言的个人目标。

If you want to become super fluent and move to the country and practically leave behind your native language, you're looking at a very different time commitment than someone who wants to chat with market vendors on their vacation and know enough to get around.

如果你想达到极高的流利程度，搬到使用该语言的国家生活，并且几乎不再使用母语，那么所需的时间投入会与另一类人截然不同——后者只想在度假时和市场摊贩聊几句，并掌握足够应付出行的语言。

There's nothing wrong with either of those goals, by the way! Each person has very different reasons to learn a language.

顺便说一句，这两种目标都没有任何问题！每个人学习语言的理由都很不一样。

In our hour estimates, we assume that the goal is what we call "Functional Fluency" — being able to comfortably live your life in the target language. Functional Fluency

在我们的小时数估算中，我们假定目标是达到所谓的“实用流利”——能够自如地使用目标语言生活。实用流利

That means you can understand media pretty easily, have conversations on topics you know something about, and can communicate without issue. You will still sound like a foreigner and make plenty of mistakes.

这意味着你能比较轻松地理解媒体内容，围绕自己熟悉的话题进行交谈，并且可以顺畅沟通。你听起来仍会像外国人，也仍会犯不少错误。

If your goal is higher or lower than this, keep that in mind. For someone who just wants to make comfortable small talk, around 50% of the functional fluency number is probably more accurate. If you want to go to university and immigrate to a country that uses that language, maybe multiply the number by 1.5.

如果你的目标高于或低于这一水平，请把这种差异考虑在内。对于只想轻松闲聊的人，实用流利小时数的 50% 左右或许更准确。如果你想去大学深造，并移民到使用该语言的国家，那么可能需要把这个数字乘以 1.5。

Total Hour Estimates总小时数估算

Alright, here's the part you've been waiting for. If you scrolled down to this section without reading above, you're missing out on valuable nuance that will help you understand these numbers.

好了，下面就是你一直在等的部分。如果你没有阅读前文，直接滑到了这一节，就会错过许多有价值的细节，而这些细节能帮助你正确理解这些数字。

And one final reminder: These numbers are meant to be estimates to help you plan your language learning journey.

最后再提醒一次：这些数字只是估算值，旨在帮助你规划自己的语言学习之旅。

How long does it take to reach functional fluency in a…

一门……语言需要多长时间才能达到实用流利？

  • Cousin language: 800 hours
  • Similar language: 1,350 hours
  • Other language: 1,700 hours
  • Distant language: 2,700 hours
  • 近亲语言：800 小时
  • 相近语言：1,350 小时
  • 其他语言：1,700 小时
  • 远缘语言：2,700 小时

The "And Beyond" phase doesn't have numbers since it goes on forever! After you reach functional fluency, unless you totally drop the language forever, you'll continuously learn more for the rest of your life.

“更进一步”阶段没有对应数字，因为这个阶段永远不会结束！达到实用流利后，只要你没有彻底放弃这门语言，此后余生都会不断学到更多。

Let's look at a few examples.

我们来看几个例子。

Let's say you're a native English speaker learning German — that's a similar language. You don't actually want to reach functional fluency; you just want to have basic conversations. So your goal is to finish the speaking phase (which roughly equates to "small talk fluency"). Your target is 900 hours in German.

假设你的母语是英语，正在学习德语——这是一门相近语言。你其实并不想达到实用流利，只想进行基本对话。因此，你的目标是完成口语阶段（大致相当于“流利闲聊”）。那么，你学习德语的目标就是 900 小时。

If you're a native English speaker learning Japanese, and your goal is to go beyond functional fluency, you're looking at aiming for closer to 3,500 hours (and then continuing from there).

如果你的母语是英语，正在学习日语，并且目标是超越实用流利，那么你应该以接近 3,500 小时为目标（之后再继续学习）。

But, don't think about that big, final number!

但是，不要想着那个庞大的最终数字！

Reaching the "Fun Threshold"达到“乐趣门槛”

Instead, you should focus on reaching the "fun threshold." This is the point where learning stops feeling so much like learning. You're good enough at the language that immersion and constant learning become fun — something you look forward to doing.

你应该把注意力放在达到“乐趣门槛”上。到了这个节点，学习就不再那么像是在学习。你的语言水平已经足够好，沉浸和持续学习开始变得有趣——成为一件让你期待去做的事。

The fun threshold comes at around 20% of the functional fluency estimate.

乐趣门槛大约出现在实用流利估算时数的 20%左右。

Don't think about how it'll take you 1,700 hours to become fluent in Russian. Focus on reaching 340 hours.

不要想着要花 1,700 小时才能流利使用俄语。先专注于达到 340 小时。

How long does it take to reach the fun threshold in a…

要达到能够享受语言乐趣的门槛，需要多长时间？对于……

  • Cousin language: 160 hours
  • Similar language: 270 hours
  • Other language: 340 hours
  • Distant language: 540 hours
  • 近亲语言：160 小时
  • 相似语言：270 小时
  • 其他语言：340 小时
  • 远亲语言：540 小时

If you were to learn for 2 hours per day (which is a lot, but doable if you incorporate it into your day well), the fun threshold will take you:

如果你每天学习 2 小时（这个时长不少，但只要合理融入日常生活，就能做到），那么达到能够享受语言乐趣的门槛大约需要：

  • about 3 months for a cousin language
  • about 4.5 months for a similar language
  • about 6 months for an other language
  • about 9 months for a distant language
  • 近亲语言约需 3 个月
  • 相似语言约需 4.5 个月
  • 其他语言约需 6 个月
  • 远亲语言约需 9 个月

Once you can have fun in a language, you're never going to want to stop learning!

一旦你能从一门语言中获得乐趣，就再也不会想停止学习！

Slow and Steady Wins the Race稳扎稳打才能赢得长跑

Language learning is not a sprint. You can't jam 2,000 hours into a few months. Not only are there only 24 hours in a day, but your brain can't absorb more than a few hours of a new language per day. We don't know if there's really a limit, but learners start to lose effectiveness beyond 5 hours per day.

语言学习不是短跑。你不可能把 2,000 小时的学习硬塞进短短几个月。一天不仅只有 24 小时，而且你的大脑每天也只能吸收几个小时的新语言内容。我们不知道是否真的存在明确上限，但每天超过 5 小时后，学习者的效率就会开始下降。

It's a much better approach to go slow and steady. Focus on creating a language learning routine that you can maintain every day, without fail. Habit Health

更好的方法是稳步前进。专注于建立一套自己每天都能雷打不动地坚持下去的语言学习习惯。习惯健康度

The most common reason people fail to learn a language is burnout. Their first week or month is incredibly strong… but then it becomes too much and falls apart.

人们没能学会一门语言，最常见的原因就是精疲力竭。他们在第一周或第一个月冲劲十足……但之后负担越来越重，最终再也坚持不下去。

Start with 45 minutes per day. Then when that's solid, increase it to an hour. And then an hour and a half. And in no time, you'll be learning for almost 2 hours every day!

先从每天 45 分钟开始。等这个习惯稳定下来，再增加到一小时，然后增加到一个半小时。用不了多久，你每天就能学习将近 2 小时了！

Where Do These Numbers Come From?这些数字从何而来？

The most commonly cited hour estimates for language learning come from the Foreign Service Institute (FSI), which teaches English native speakers foreign languages so they can work in foreign affairs. They have a lot of experience teaching languages to people.

语言学习领域最常引用的学习时长估算来自美国外交学院（FSI）。该学院教授英语母语者外语，以便他们从事外交事务，因此在语言教学方面经验丰富。

They split languages into 4 categories based on difficulty (though they're a bit different from ours) and give an estimate for how long it takes a learner to reach Level 3 on the ILR scale.

他们按照难度将语言分为 4 类（尽管分类方式与我们略有不同），并估算学习者达到 ILR 量表 3 级所需的时间。

They make their estimates in terms of number of weeks and classroom hours required. This is because their language courses are fairly standardized and the people they teach are learning full-time.

他们以所需周数和课堂学时来进行估算，因为其语言课程相当标准化，而且学员都是全职学习。

The time estimates from the FSI are very frequently incorrectly cited since the hours they list on their website are only classroom hours. But students are expected to also do nearly as much self-study every week.

FSI 的时间估算经常被错误引用，因为其网站上列出的时长仅仅是课堂学时。然而，学生每周还需要投入几乎同样多的时间进行自主学习。

A typical week is 23 hours per week in class and 17 hours of self-study. That means these students are learning for 40 hours per week — a full-time job.

典型的一周包括 23 小时课堂学习和 17 小时自主学习。也就是说，这些学生每周学习 40 小时——相当于一份全职工作。

These estimates from the Foreign Service Institute are very good, and we used them for quite a while whenever we talked about time to learn a language.

美国外交学院的这些估算非常可靠，很长一段时间里，我们在讨论学会一门语言需要多久时都会采用它们。

However, they don't match up perfectly with immersion learning or the Refold method.

不过，它们与沉浸学习或 Refold 方法并不完全吻合。

The primary difference is that immersion learning works a little differently. In an FSI course, nearly 60% of your time with the language is in a classroom. This is great for explicitly learning grammar rules and following a textbook, but it's not the same as focused, interactive immersion time.

主要区别在于沉浸学习的运作方式略有不同。在 FSI 课程中，你接触语言的时间有近 60% 是在课堂上度过的。这非常适合系统学习语法规则和按教材推进，但与专注、互动式的沉浸时间并不相同。

The way our brains acquire language from immersion learning is very different from classroom learning. People in our community tend to progress a little faster in the more difficult languages (what the FSI calls Category 3 and 4, and what we call distant languages).

我们的大脑通过沉浸学习习得语言的方式，与课堂学习截然不同。对于难度较高的语言（FSI 所称的第 3 类和第 4 类，也就是我们所说的远亲语言），我们社区的学习者往往进步得稍快一些。

The other main difference is that we group languages differently. We add the easiest category of "cousin languages" (the FSI doesn't have this, since English pretty much doesn't have any) and we shuffle some of the Category 2 languages around. The experience of learning German in a classroom, for example, makes it feel harder than it is for someone learning through immersion.

另一个主要区别是我们的语言分类方式不同。我们增加了最简单的“近亲语言”类别（FSI 没有这一类，因为英语基本上没有近亲语言），并调整了部分第 2 类语言的归属。例如，在课堂上学习德语的体验会让它显得比实际更难；对于通过沉浸学习的人而言，并没有那么困难。

Our hour estimates are based on the Foreign Service Institute's decades of experience, but modified to better match the experience of our immersion learning community of over 70,000 learners.

我们的时长估算以美国外交学院数十年的经验为基础，但也进行了调整，以便更贴合我们这个拥有超过 70,000 名学习者的沉浸学习社区的实际体验。

And the "per phase" breakdown of hours is based on a few people's experience and our best estimates. When we created the 7-phase system from the original 4-stage one, we looked at where people in our community were spending the most time and estimated from there.

至于“各阶段”的时长分配，则是根据少数人的经验和我们的最佳估算得出的。当我们把原来的 4 阶段体系改为 7 阶段体系时，考察了社区成员在哪些阶段花费的时间最多，并据此进行了估算。
